Profitable repeat orders, with merchant control

Estimate when an existing customer may be ready to reorder. Adapt to their reply, prepare the same-product checkout, arrange a reminder, or hand off an issue.

Problem

Fixed reminders cannot tell whether a customer needs more time, has a price concern, or has an unresolved issue.

How it works

  1. Find the repeat opportunityUse permitted purchase history and customer-stated dates.
  2. Work within your approved planCheck eligibility, contact limits and channel permissions.
  3. Adapt and record the outcomeReorder, reschedule or route to support. Verify purchase evidence.

Capabilities and limits

Same-SKU checkout, reminders and support handoffs. No new discounts, substitute products or automatic charges.
